### 01. Completed the mathematical formulation and machine model

**When / state:** By 28 October; explanations expanded on 29 October. **Completed.**  
**Report:** 1(a–e), 2(a–d).

- Defined the operation as batch-one dense FP32 1×1 convolution plus optional bias, with the public NCHW input/output contract.
- Expressed an output tile as a sequence of outer-product updates over input channels, retaining each output's accumulation order.
- Derived eight FP32 values per 256-bit register, 16 FLOP per vector FMA, and 32 FLOP/core-cycle from two FMA issue slots.
- Identified five-cycle FMA recurrence latency and the resulting ten-accumulator-chain requirement.

**What this established:** A numerical target and resource model against which to design the kernel. It did not establish an achieved runtime or sustained CPU frequency.

**Evidence:** [Public contract](code/reference/include/p2.h), [tile reasoning](archives/project_support/docs/design.md#register-tiles), [Broadwell instruction records](archives/project_support/evidence/broadwell_instructions.json), [calculation source, S1–S2](report/calculations.tex).

### 02. Derived the output shape and register budget

**When / state:** Initial design by 28 October; full worked derivation added on 29 October. **Completed.**  
**Report:** 2(d), 3(a–d).

- Defined N = 8V spatial positions and R = MV + V + 1 live vector registers.
- Applied the ten-chain requirement, load-port bound, and 16-register limit. For V = 2, these give N = 16 and 5 ≤ M ≤ 6.
- Calculated 5×16 as 80 outputs, ten accumulators, 13 total YMM, 160 FLOP per reduction step, and a five-cycle ideal body bound.
- Calculated 6×16 as 96 outputs, twelve accumulators, 15 total YMM, 192 FLOP per reduction step, and a six-cycle ideal body bound.
- Enumerated 30 register-feasible source tiles rather than assuming one shape wins.

**What this established:** Both focused tiles have the same modeled 32 FLOP/cycle ceiling. Six channels have better reuse/amortization, but the model does not prove they are always faster.

**Evidence:** [Tile design space](archives/project_support/docs/tile_design_space.csv), [variant definitions](code/configs/variants.json), [worked calculations, S2–S3](report/calculations.tex).
